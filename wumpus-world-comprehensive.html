Wumpus World

Artificial Intelligence — Logical Agents

The Wumpus World Environment

The Wumpus World is a classic AI benchmark environment — a grid of rooms containing hazards and treasure. It illustrates how a knowledge-based agent can reason with partial information.

Environment Setup
4 × 4 grid of rooms, connected by passages
Agent starts at [1,1] (bottom-left). One Wumpus, multiple pits, one piece of gold placed randomly.

Key Elements

Wumpus
A creature that eats anyone who enters its room. Can be killed by shooting an arrow into its room.
Pits
Deadly traps in some rooms. Agent dies if it enters a pit room.
Gold
The goal — agent receives +1000 for finding and grabbing it.
Agent
Starts at [1,1] — this room is always safe (no pit, no wumpus).
Challenge: The agent cannot directly see the Wumpus, pits, or gold. It must infer their locations from indirect sensor readings — a classic example of reasoning under partial observability.

Why Wumpus World?

  • Simple but non-trivial: Easy to describe but requires logical reasoning
  • Partial observability: Agent must infer hidden state from indirect clues
  • Sequential reasoning: Information gathered at different times/locations must be combined
  • Unfair environments: ~21% of environments are "unfair" (gold in a pit or surrounded by pits)
  • Classic benchmark: Used to test reasoning algorithms since the 1980s

Actuators Forward — move one square in the direction the agent faces

TurnRight(90°) — rotate clockwise

Shoot — fire arrow in the direction facing (kills wumpus if it's in that direction)
Sensors 5-tuple percept: [Stench, Breeze, Glitter, Bump, Scream]
Stench — perceived in rooms adjacent to the Wumpus
Breeze — perceived in rooms adjacent to a pit
Glitter — perceived in the room containing gold
Bump — perceived when walking into a wall
Scream — perceived when the Wumpus is killed (heard everywhere)

Percept Format

Percept Vector
Percept = [Stench, Breeze, Glitter, Bump, Scream]
Each value is either True or False. Example: [True, False, False, False, False] means stench detected, no breeze, no glitter, no bump, no scream.

Sensor Diagram

Sensor Mapping
Wumpus at [x,y]: Stench at [x-1,y], [x+1,y], [x,y-1], [x,y+1] (if within bounds) Pit at [x,y]: Breeze at [x-1,y], [x+1,y], [x,y-1], [x,y+1] (if within bounds) Gold at [x,y]: Glitter only at [x,y] Arrow kills Wumpus: Scream heard everywhere (one-time event)
Example: Percept Interpretation

Scenario: Agent is at [2,1] and receives percept [False, True, False, False, False]

Interpretation:

  • Stench = False → No Wumpus in [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1] (adjacent squares)
  • Breeze = True → There IS a pit in [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1]
  • But [1,1] is the start room (known safe) → pit must be in [2,2] or [3,1]
  • Glitter = False → no gold here
  • Bump = False → didn't hit a wall
  • Scream = False → Wumpus is still alive

Step-by-Step Reasoning Walkthrough

This is the canonical Wumpus World reasoning example showing how the agent infers safe squares from its percepts over time.

Initial State: Agent at [1,1]

Step 1: First Percept (Start Room)

Percept at [1,1]: [None, None, None, None, None] — no stench, no breeze, no glitter, no bump, no scream

Inference:

  • No stench → Wumpus is NOT in [1,2] or [2,1] (adjacent squares)
  • No breeze → Pit is NOT in [1,2] or [2,1]
  • Conclusion: Both [1,2] and [2,1] are safe

Action: Move to [2,1] (or [1,2]) — the agent chooses [2,1]

Step 2: Second Room [2,1]

Percept at [2,1]: [None, Breeze, None, None, None] — breeze detected!

Inference:

  • Breeze → pit exists in one of [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1]
  • [1,1] is already known safe → pit is in [2,2] OR [3,1]
  • No stench → no Wumpus in [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1]
  • Conclusion so far: Pit at [2,2] or [3,1]; Wumpus location unknown

Action: Return to [1,1] (safe), then go to [1,2]

Step 3: Room [1,2]

Percept at [1,2]: [Stench, None, None, None, None] — stench detected!

Inference:

  • Stench → Wumpus is in [1,1], [1,3], or [2,2]
  • [1,1] is the start room (no stench before) → Wumpus NOT in [1,1]
  • When at [2,1], there was no stench → Wumpus NOT in [2,2]
  • Conclusion: Wumpus MUST be in [1,3]!

Additional Inference:

  • No breeze at [1,2] → no pit in [1,1], [1,3], or [2,2]
  • Earlier we concluded pit is in [2,2] or [3,1]
  • Since [2,2] has no pit → pit MUST be in [3,1]!

Action: Go to [2,2] (now known to be safe)

Step 4: Room [2,2] and Beyond

Percept at [2,2]: [None, None, None, None, None] — no stench, no breeze

Inference:

  • No stench → no Wumpus in [1,2], [2,3], [3,2], or [2,1] (all consistent)
  • No breeze → no pit in [1,2], [2,3], [3,2], or [2,1] (all consistent)
  • [2,3] and [3,2] are now known safe!

Action: Proceed to [2,3]

Step 5: Gold Found!

Percept at [2,3]: [None, None, Glitter, None, None] — GOLD!

Inference: Gold is in this room. The goal is reached.

Action: Grab gold, then navigate back to [1,1] to exit.

Output: Agent successfully found gold and returned to start! Total score: +1000 - (number of actions × 1).

Different Wumpus World Scenarios

Example 1: Wumpus Adjacent to Start

Setup: Wumpus at [1,2], gold at [2,2], no pits

Step 1: Agent at [1,1] — percept [Stench, None, None, None, None]

Inference: Stench means Wumpus in [1,2] or [2,1]. Both are adjacent. Agent cannot be sure which one yet.

Step 2: Move to [2,1] — percept [None, None, None, None, None] (no stench, no breeze)

Inference: No stench at [2,1] → Wumpus not in [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1]. Since Wumpus must be in [1,2] or [2,1], and [2,1] is clear → Wumpus at [1,2].

Step 3: Cannot go to [1,2] (Wumpus there). Shoot arrow at [1,2] — if Scream heard, Wumpus is dead.

Step 4: Enter [1,2], then [2,2] to grab gold.

Output: Agent shot the Wumpus and retrieved gold safely.

Example 2: Multiple Pits with Gold in Corner

Setup: Pits at [2,1] and [3,2]; Wumpus at [4,4]; gold at [4,3]

Step 1: [1,1] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [1,2] and [2,1] are safe

Step 2: Move to [1,2] — [None, None, None, None, None] → safe; [1,3] and [2,2] are safe

Step 3: Move to [2,2] — [None, None, None, None, None] → safe; [2,3] and [3,2] and [2,1] safe

Step 4: Move to [2,3] — [None, Breeze, None, None, None] → pit in [1,3], [2,4], or [3,3]

Inference: [1,3] was visited earlier (no breeze) → pit in [2,4] or [3,3]

Step 5: Move to [3,3] — [None, Breeze, None, None, None] → pit in [2,3], [3,2], [3,4], or [4,3]. [2,3] visited (no pit) → [3,2] or [3,4] or [4,3]

Step 6: Move to [4,3] — [None, None, Glitter, None, None] → GOLD!

Output: Agent navigated through a minefield of pits to reach the gold.

Example 3: Wumpus on the Path — Agent Must Shoot

Setup: Wumpus at [2,2]; pit at [3,1]; gold at [4,4]

Step 1: [1,1] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [1,2] and [2,1] safe

Step 2: [2,1] — [None, Breeze, None, None, None] → pit in [2,2] or [3,1]

Step 3: Return to [1,1]; go to [1,2] — [Stench, None, None, None, None] → Wumpus in [1,1], [1,3], or [2,2]. [1,1] known safe → [1,3] or [2,2]

Step 4: No breeze at [1,2] → no pit in [1,3] or [2,2]. Earlier [2,2] or [3,1] had pit. Since [2,2] is clear → pit at [3,1].

Step 5: Wumpus in [1,3] or [2,2]. [1,2] has stench (Wumpus adjacent) → [1,3] or [2,2] or [1,1]. [1,1] no stench. Move to [2,2]... if stench at [2,2] → Wumpus at [2,2]. Shoot!

Step 6: After killing Wumpus, proceed to [2,3], [2,4], [3,4], [4,4] for gold.

Output: Agent killed the Wumpus blocking the path and reached the gold.

Example 4: Unfair Environment — No Safe Path

Setup: Gold at [2,2], pits at [1,2] and [2,1] — the gold is surrounded by pits.

Step 1: [1,1] — [None, Breeze, None, None, None] → pit in [1,2] or [2,1]

Both adjacent rooms have pits! The agent cannot reach the gold without a path.

Possible strategies:

  • Give up: return to [1,1] and exit with score = -(actions taken)
  • Risk: there's ~21% chance the environment is unfair — best to cut losses

Output: Unfair environment detected — agent chose to exit with minimal losses rather than risk death.

Example 5: Wumpus in Corner, Gold in Center

Setup: Wumpus at [1,4]; pits at [3,1] and [4,2]; gold at [3,3]

Step 1: [1,1] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [1,2] and [2,1] safe

Step 2: [2,1] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [2,2] and [3,1] safe (no breeze)

Wait — no breeze at [2,1] means no pit in [1,1], [2,2], or [3,1]. But we only knew [1,1] safe. Now [2,2] and [3,1] are also safe.

Step 3: [2,2] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [2,3] and [3,2] safe

Step 4: [3,2] — [None, None, None, None, None] → [3,3] and [4,2] safe

Step 5: [3,3] — [None, None, Glitter, None, None] → GOLD!

Output: Agent found a clear path to gold by systematically exploring safe squares.

Strategy & Analysis

Agent Decision Making

The Wumpus World agent must decide between exploration and exploitation:

SituationActionRationale
Known safe unexplored room Go there Gather more information; find gold
Gold glitter detected Grab gold, then exit Primary goal achieved
Wumpus location known Avoid it or shoot it Remove threat if path is blocked
No safe unexplored rooms Exit (return to [1,1]) Cannot achieve goal; minimize losses
Certain death implied Exit immediately Risk-averse strategy for unfair envs

Logical Reasoning Complexity

Number of possible worlds
With 16 rooms × 3 features (pit/wumpus/gold), the state space is enormous. Logical inference prunes this dramatically.
Inference difficulty
Combining information from different times and locations is cognitively demanding — most animals cannot do it, but logical agents can.
Soundness guarantee
If the initial knowledge is correct and inference rules are sound, every derived conclusion is guaranteed true in the real world.
Key Learning
Wumpus World demonstrates: Knowledge + Inference = Intelligent Action under Uncertainty
The agent never directly sees the Wumpus, pits, or gold — yet it can act intelligently by combining partial information over time.
Important: The reasoning shown here is logical deduction — the agent doesn't guess. Each conclusion is guaranteed true if the premises are true. This is the foundation of logical AI.
