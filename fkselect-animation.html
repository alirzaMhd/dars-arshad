Problem Setup

Given n items with profits and weights, fill a knapsack of capacity M to maximize total profit. Items can be taken fractionally.

The FKSelect algorithm uses a median-of-ratios partitioning strategy to solve this in O(n) average time per level of recursion.

Key Insight

Sort by ratio p[i]/w[i] descending → greedy optimal

FKSelect avoids full sorting by partitioning around the median ratio, similar to QuickSelect.
