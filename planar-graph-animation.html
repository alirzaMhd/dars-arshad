Planar Graph Theory

A planar graph can be drawn on a plane without any edges crossing.

Euler's Formula: For any connected planar graph: V − E + F = 2

Edge Bound: For V ≥ 3: E ≤ 3V − 6

Kuratowski's Theorem: A graph is planar iff it contains no subdivision of K5 or K3,3.
