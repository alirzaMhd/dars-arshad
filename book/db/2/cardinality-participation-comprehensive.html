CARDINALITY & PARTICIPATION

Constraints & (min,max) Notation — Complete Guide for Exam

Cardinality Ratios

Definition:
Cardinality = The number of entity instances that can participate in a relationship
Determines the maximum number of associations

Four Types of Cardinality

One-to-One (1:1)

At most one on each side

Person ↔ Passport

One-to-Many (1:n)

One on left, many on right

Department → Employees

Many-to-One (n:1)

Many on left, one on right

Students → Department

Many-to-Many (m:n)

Many on both sides

Students ↔ Courses

How to Determine:
Read both directions: "A has [how many] B" AND "B has [how many] A"
Example: "One department has many employees" AND "Each employee works in one department" = 1:n

Participation Constraints

Definition:
Participation = Whether all entity instances must participate in a relationship
Total = Mandatory, Partial = Optional

Types of Participation

Total (Mandatory)

Every entity MUST participate

Notation: Double line in ER

e.g., Every Employee must work in a Department

Partial (Optional)

Some entities MAY NOT participate

Notation: Single line in ER

e.g., Some Employees may not manage a Department

Examples

Total: Every Student MUST enroll in at least one Course

Partial: Some Courses MAY NOT have any Students enrolled

Total: Every Dependent MUST have an Owner Employee

Partial: Some Employees MAY NOT have any Dependents

Key Point

Total participation means the entity is existence-dependent on the relationship. Weak entities always have total participation in their identifying relationship.

(min, max) Notation

Notation:
(min, max) for each entity's participation
min = minimum (0=partial, 1=total), max = maximum (1=at most one, n=many)

Four Combinations

Notation Meaning Participation Cardinality
(0,1) Optional, at most one Partial One
(1,1) Mandatory, exactly one Total One
(0,n) Optional, many Partial Many
(1,n) Mandatory, at least one Total Many
Example: Employee Works_In Department

Employee(1,1): Each employee works in exactly one department (total, one)

Department(0,n): Each department has 0 or more employees (partial, many)

Reading: Employee side is mandatory (must have dept), Department side is optional (can have no employees)

Example: Student Enrolls_In Course

Student(1,n): Each student takes at least one course (total, many)

Course(1,m): Each course has at least one student (total, many)

Examples & Comparison

Example 1: University System

Student(1,n) --- takes --- Course(1,m)

Relationship Attribute: Grade

Relational: Enrollment(Student_ID, Course_ID, Grade)

Example 2: Company System

Employee(1,1) --- works_in --- Department(0,n)

Relational: Employee(Emp_ID, Name, Dept_ID)

Example 3: Hospital System

Doctor(0,n) --- treats --- Patient(0,n)

Relational: Treatment(Doctor_ID, Patient_ID, Diagnosis)

Comparison Table

Constraint min max Participation Cardinality
Optional, One 0 1 Partial One
Mandatory, One 1 1 Total One
Optional, Many 0 n Partial Many
Mandatory, Many 1 n Total Many

Conversion Rules

To Relational Schema:
1:1 → FK in either table (with UNIQUE)
1:n → FK in many side, m:n → new junction table
Exam Tip

When converting (min,max) to relational: If min=1 (total participation), make the FK NOT NULL.
