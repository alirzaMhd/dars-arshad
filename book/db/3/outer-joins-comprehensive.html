OUTER JOINS

Database Systems — Preserving Unmatched Tuples for Exam Preparation

Left Outer Join (⟕)

The left outer join returns all tuples from the left relation, and matching tuples from the right relation. Unmatched right tuples get NULL.

Definition:
R ⟕ S = (R ⋈ S) ∪ (R - π_R.common(R ⋈ S)) × {(NULL,...,NULL)}
Natural join plus left-unmatched tuples with NULLs for right attributes.

Right Outer Join (⟖)

The right outer join returns all tuples from the right relation, and matching tuples from the left relation. Unmatched left tuples get NULL.

Definition:
R ⟖ S = S ⟕ R
Right outer join of R and S is equivalent to left outer join of S and R.
Example 2: Right Outer Join

Employee ⟖ Department:

EmpIDEmployee.DeptIDDeptName
E1D1Engineering
E2D2Marketing

Department D4 (Sales) has no employees, so EmpID is NULL.

Full Outer Join (⟗)

The full outer join returns all tuples from both relations. Unmatched tuples get NULLs for missing attributes.

Definition:
R ⟗ S = (R ⟕ S) ∪ (R ⟖ S)
Union of left and right outer joins.
Example 3: Full Outer Join

Employee ⟗ Department:

EmpIDEmployee.DeptIDDeptName

E2D2Marketing

All employees and all departments are included, with NULLs where there's no match.

When to Use Each

Use CaseRecommended Join
Only want matching dataInner Join (⋈)
Keep all from left tableLeft Outer Join (⟕)

Key Rules to Remember

✓ Left outer join preserves all left tuples

✓ Right outer join preserves all right tuples

✓ Full outer join preserves all tuples from both

✓ Unmatched tuples get NULL values

✓ Inner join is a subset of outer joins
