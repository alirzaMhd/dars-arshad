TOTAL & PARTIAL PARTICIPATION

Database Systems — Entity Participation Constraints for Exam Preparation

Total Participation (Mandatory)

Total participation means that every entity instance must participate in the relationship.

Definition:
∀e ∈ E, ∃r ∈ R such that e participates in r
Every entity in E must appear in at least one relationship instance in R.
Example 1: Total Participation

Given: Employee WORKS_FOR Department

Constraint: Total participation of Employee

  • Every Employee MUST work for some Department
  • An Employee cannot exist without being assigned to a Department

Notation: Double line from Employee to WORKS_FOR relationship

Total Participation Means:

✓ Every entity must participate

✓ Cannot have an entity without the relationship

✓ Often corresponds to NOT NULL constraint on FK

Partial Participation (Optional)

Partial participation means that some entity instances may not participate in the relationship.

Definition:
∃e ∈ E such that ¬(∃r ∈ R where e participates in r)
There exists at least one entity in E that does not participate in any relationship instance.
Example 2: Partial Participation

Given: Employee MANAGES Department

Constraint: Partial participation of Department

  • Some Departments may not have a manager (yet)
  • A Department can exist without a manager

Notation: Single line from Department to MANAGES relationship

Cardinality Ratios

1:1 (One-to-One)

Each entity in E₁ relates to at most one in E₂, and vice versa

Example: Person HAS Passport

1:N (One-to-Many)

Each entity in E₁ relates to many in E₂, but each E₂ relates to at most one E₁

Example: Department HAS Employees

M:N (Many-to-Many)

Each entity in E₁ can relate to many in E₂, and vice versa

Example: Student ENROLLS_IN Course

Example 6: 1:M Relationship

One Department has many Employees, each Employee works for one Department:

Total participation of Employee: Every employee must have a department

Partial participation of Department: Some departments may have no employees

CREATE TABLE Employee (
  EmpID INT PRIMARY KEY,
  DeptID INT NOT NULL, -- Total participation
  FOREIGN KEY (DeptID) REFERENCES Department(DeptID)
);

Key Rules to Remember

✓ Total: every entity must participate

✓ Partial: some entities may not participate

✓ Total participation → NOT NULL constraint

✓ Partial participation → NULL allowed

✓ Cardinality and participation are independent concepts
