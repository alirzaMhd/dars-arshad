AVL Tree Construction

Data Structures — Complete Guide for 20/20 Exam

Four Rotation Cases

When imbalance occurs after insertion:
Find the first unbalanced node z, then determine case based on where the new node was inserted
Let y be the child of z on the path to the new node
Let x be the grandchild of z on the path to the new node

LL Case (Left-Left)

• x is left child of y

• y is left child of z

• Fix: Right Rotate(z)

RR Case (Right-Right)

• x is right child of y

• y is right child of z

• Fix: Left Rotate(z)

LR Case (Left-Right)

• x is right child of y

• y is left child of z

• Fix: Left Rotate(y) then Right Rotate(z)

RL Case (Right-Left)

• x is left child of y

• y is right child of z

• Fix: Right Rotate(y) then Left Rotate(z)

Key Point: After any insertion, at most ONE rotation (or two for LR/RL) is needed to restore balance. This is why AVL insertion is O(lg n).

LL Case (Left-Left Imbalance)

When it occurs:
New node inserted in LEFT subtree of LEFT child
Tree becomes left-heavy, needs right rotation
Example: Insert 5 into this AVL tree
Before insertion (balanced): 30 (BF=1) / \ 20 40 / 10 Insert 5 (new node): 30 (BF=2) ← IMBALANCED! / \ 20 40 (BF=0) / \ 10 5 Fix: Right Rotate on 30 After rotation: 20 (BF=0) / \ 10 30 / \ / \ 5 NIL NIL 40

• z = 30 (first unbalanced node)

• y = 20 (z's left child)

• x = 10 or 5 (on the path)

• Fix: Right Rotate(30)

RR Case (Right-Right Imbalance)

When it occurs:
New node inserted in RIGHT subtree of RIGHT child
Tree becomes right-heavy, needs left rotation
Example: Insert 45 into this AVL tree
Before insertion (balanced): 20 (BF=-1) / \ 10 30 \ 40 Insert 45 (new node): 20 (BF=-2) ← IMBALANCED! / \ 10 30 (BF=-1) \ 40 \ 45 Fix: Left Rotate on 20 After rotation: 30 (BF=0) / \ 20 40 / \ \ 10 NIL 45

• z = 20 (first unbalanced node)

• y = 30 (z's right child)

• x = 40 or 45 (on the path)

• Fix: Left Rotate(20)

LR and RL Cases (Double Rotation)

LR Case:
Left Rotate(y) then Right Rotate(z)
New node in RIGHT subtree of LEFT child
LR Case Example
Before: After Left Rotate(10): 30 (BF=2) 30 (BF=2) / / \ 10 15 40 \ 15 After Right Rotate(30): 15 (BF=0) / \ 10 30
RL Case:
Right Rotate(y) then Left Rotate(z)
New node in LEFT subtree of RIGHT child
RL Case Example
Before: After Right Rotate(30): 10 (BF=-2) 10 (BF=-2) / \ / \ NIL 30 NIL 25 / \ 25 30 After Left Rotate(10): 25 (BF=0) / \ 10 30

Complete Worked Example

Build AVL tree from keys: 30, 20, 40, 10, 25, 35, 50, 5

Step 1: Insert 30, 20, 40

30 (BF=0) / \ 20 40

Step 2: Insert 10

30 (BF=1) / \ 20 40 / 10

Step 3: Insert 25

30 (BF=1) After inserting 25: / \ 20 40 30 (BF=2) ← IMBALANCED! / / \ 10 20 40 \ 25 Fix: LR case (left child, right subtree) Left Rotate(20) then Right Rotate(30) 25 (BF=0) / \ 20 30 / \ 10 40

Step 4: Insert 35, 50

25 (BF=-1) / \ 20 30 (BF=-1) / \ 10 35 \ 40 \ 50

Step 5: Insert 5

25 (BF=0) ← Need to check balance! / \ 20 30 / \ \ 10 5 35 \ 40 \ 50 After rebalancing: (complex rotations needed)

Quick Reference Card

LL Case

• Left of Left

• Fix: Right Rotate(z)

• 1 rotation

RR Case

• Right of Right

• Fix: Left Rotate(z)

• 1 rotation

LR Case

• Right of Left

• Fix: LR(z) then RR(z)

• 2 rotations

RL Case

• Left of Right

• Fix: RR(y) then LR(z)

• 2 rotations

Time Complexity

• Insert: O(lg n)

• Delete: O(lg n)

• Search: O(lg n)

Key Properties

• Height: O(lg n)

• Rotations: O(1)

• Always balanced

AVL Insert Algorithm:
1. Insert like normal BST
2. Walk back up to root
3. Check balance factor at each node
4. If |BF| > 1, apply appropriate rotation(s)
At most 2 rotations needed for any insertion
Exam Tip: When asked to build an AVL tree, always show: (1) the tree after each insertion, (2) identify which case (LL/RR/LR/RL), (3) show the rotation(s) applied.
