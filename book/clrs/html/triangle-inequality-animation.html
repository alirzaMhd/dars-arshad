Triangle Inequality in Graphs

The triangle inequality for shortest paths states:

δ(s, v) ≤ δ(s, u) + w(u, v)  for every edge (u, v)

Where δ(s, x) is the shortest path distance from source s to node x, and w(u, v) is the edge weight.

Why It Matters

If this inequality is violated for any edge, then δ(s, v) can be improved via u. This is the basis of edge relaxation in Bellman-Ford and Dijkstra's algorithms.
