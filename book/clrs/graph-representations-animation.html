Graph Representations

A graph G = (V, E) can be stored in two standard ways:

Adjacency-list: Array Adj of |V| lists; Adj[u] contains all vertices v such that (u, v) ∈ E. Memory: Θ(V + E).

Adjacency-matrix: |V| × |V| matrix A where aij = 1 if (i, j) ∈ E. Memory: Θ(V²).
