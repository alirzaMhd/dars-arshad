Graph Square: G2

The square of a directed graph G = (V, E) is the graph G2 = (V, E2) where:

(u, v) ∈ E2 iff G contains a path with at most 2 edges between u and v

That means (u, v) ∈ E2 if either:

• Direct edge: (u, v) ∈ E (path of length 1)

• Two-step path: ∃w such that (u, w) ∈ E and (w, v) ∈ E (path of length 2)
