Graph Transpose: GT

The transpose of a directed graph G = (V, E) is the graph GT = (V, ET) where every edge is reversed:

ET = {(v, u) : (u, v) ∈ E}

G has |V| = 5 vertices and |E| = 6 directed edges.

E = {(0→1), (0→3), (1→2), (1→3), (2→4), (3→4)}

As we process each edge, we add the reverse to GT.

GT has the same vertices and number of edges as G. The adjacency lists are swapped: AdjGT[v] = {u : (u, v) ∈ E}.
