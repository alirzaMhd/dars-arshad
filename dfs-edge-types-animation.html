DFS Edge Classification

Depth-First Search classifies every edge into one of four types based on vertex colors during traversal:

• Tree — to a white (undiscovered) vertex; forms the DFS tree

• Back — to a gray (ancestor) vertex; reveals a cycle

• Forward — to a black descendant vertex (already finished)

• Cross — to a black vertex that is not a descendant
