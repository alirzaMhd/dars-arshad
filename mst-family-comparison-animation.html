Generic MST — Safe Edge Framework
Click a panel to see detailed explanation

The four MST algorithms all rely on the cut property: for any cut, the lightest crossing edge is safe to add.

Generic explicitly uses cuts. Kruskal grows a forest by considering edges globally. Prim grows a single tree from a root. Borůvka contracts components in parallel.

Press Play All or Step All to compare them side by side.
